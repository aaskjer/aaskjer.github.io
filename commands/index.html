Blackjack Game

  • !blackjack <bet> Deals a hand Everyone
  • !bjduel @user <bet> Challenge another chatter Everyone
  • !accept Accept Duel Everyone
  • !decline Decline Duel Everyone
  • !house [rules] [bet] Open a table Mods
  • !stats Personal Stats Everyone
  • !AllStats All Stats Everyone

Typed in chat during a game

  • hit Draw another card Everyone
  • double Double the bet, take exactly one card, then stand Everyone
  • split Split a starting pair into two hands Everyone
  • stand Stop and keep your total Everyone
  • repeat Post your last game message again Everyone
  • resettut Reset Tutorial also ResetTutorial Everyone
  • quit Cancel and take the stake back - before your first move Mods
  • insurance Cover half your bet against a dealer blackjack Everyone
  • surrender Fold and keep half the bet Everyone
  • deal Take a seat, or deal the table early Everyone
  • join <bet> Take a seat, or deal the table early Everyone

Classic Chat Gamble

  • !gamble Plays a round, with the amount the viewer names or the Default bet also !gambel Everyone
  • !SetFreeGamble Turns Free Game Mode on Mods
  • !EndFreeGamble Turns Free Game Mode off Mods
  • !gambleaddict Puts a viewer on the addicted list (add) or takes them off (remove) Mods
